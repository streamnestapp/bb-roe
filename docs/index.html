Get in a match in three steps

  1. Install

    Run BB-ROE-Setup.exe and point it to your Ring of Elysium folder from Steam.

  2. Open BB-ROE

    Use the BB-ROE shortcut and choose Play online. The server IP is already filled in.

  3. Press PLAY ONLINE

    The game opens. In the lobby, go to Play → Start Matchmaking.

Three ways to play

Play online

Join the shared online server with other players and bots. Matches rotate between Mt. Dione and Europa.

A friend's room

Join a room hosted by a friend, on your local network or over the internet.

Host my own server

Your PC runs the matches. Pick the map, mode and bots in the web panel, and invite friends.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • Ring of Elysium from Steam, 2022 build 0.2.989.0. The game is not included.
  • The launcher checks Europa_Client.exe by SHA-256. Other versions won't connect.
  • Every PC needs its own copy of the game.
